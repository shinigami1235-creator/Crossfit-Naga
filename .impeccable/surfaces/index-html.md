## Direction contract

THESIS: The naga guards the stairs of every Thai temple; this site is that staircase. Training is a climb taken one step at a time with the naga beside you. Refuses the default black gym page with a red CTA and stock athletes.

OWN-WORLD: Deep lacquer green (near-black green) and sage fields own the page, cream for type and paper panels, temple gilt (#c9a44c) for ornament only (the medallion rim and relief, the /// slashes, the climb rail), and one spark colour: the gym's neon yellow, used only for the primary action and live states. Naga scale pattern and stepped diagonal edges (from the coach cards' slashes) as the only ornament. Condensed athletic display type for names and headings, a plain humanist sans for reading.

STORY: Visitor sees the naga come alive, lands in the gym mid-session (real class footage on mobile and in the classes section), understands beginners start at step one, meets the coaches, sees the parties, checks times and prices, books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: After the intro, full-bleed class video under a deep green grade. Headline bottom-left at display scale, two buttons under it: Book a class (neon yellow) and Chat on LINE. Scale-pattern climb rail on the right edge. Nav top: logo mark, sections, TH/EN, Book.

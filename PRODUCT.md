# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JS with no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages. Three.js from a CDN for the 3D logo.

## Users

Adults in Bangkok's Thong Lo / Ekkamai area, Thai and expat, deciding whether to try a CrossFit or Hyrox class. Many are beginners worried about being out of their depth; some are experienced athletes looking for a box to drop into. Parents looking for CrossFit Kids and Teens.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for CrossFit Naga, a CrossFit and Hyrox affiliated gym. Success is a visitor booking a class on PushPress or messaging the gym on LINE.

## Positioning

A CrossFit box with a named, credentialed coaching team (six coaches with distinct specialties: Hyrox and hybrid, calisthenics and handstands, Chinese weightlifting, Pilates, CrossFit Kids), a visible social life (Halloween classes, Christmas, the 1st anniversary crowd, team trips to Thailand Throwdown), and a second branch opening in Sathorn. The mascot is the naga, a Thai serpent, drawn in a Thai temple-art style.

## Product Principles

1. Every button leads to booking or LINE.
2. Real people from the gym in every image; no stock photos.
3. Beginners must see they are welcome before they see anything intimidating.
4. Facts come from the gym's own channels and are dated where they can go stale.

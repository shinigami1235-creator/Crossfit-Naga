Your first four steps

  1. 1

    Book a class

    Pick a time on the booking calendar or message us on LINE. Drop-ins pay at the gym.

  2. 2

    Start with CrossFit Basics

    Basics runs Wednesday at 17:00 and Friday at 8:45, and covers the core movements before you join regular classes.

  3. 3

    Join the daily classes

    CrossFit runs up to five times a day on weekdays, between 6:30 and 19:15.

  4. 4

    Pick your direction

    Train for a Hyrox race, learn Olympic and Chinese weightlifting, get your first handstand, or compete with the team.

What we train

  • Strength, gymnastics and conditioning in a coached group, scaled for beginners and pushed for athletes.

    Every weekday, plus Sunday at 11:00

  • CrossFit Naga is an official HYROX gym, and the classes train running and station work for race day.

    Mon, Tue, Wed and Fri, plus Hyrox Team on Saturday and Sunday

  • Classes teach the snatch and the clean and jerk from the ground up, and Coach Hansen runs Chinese weightlifting on Saturday afternoons.

    Olympic lifting Tue 11:00, Sat and Sun 9:45. Chinese lifting Sat 15:30

  • Coach Hakim teaches handstands, pull-ups and bodyweight strength.

    Calisthenics Thu 19:30, Skills 101 Fri 11:00, Gymnastics Sat 11:00

  • CrossFit Kids with Coach Annika, booked separately from adult classes, and CrossFit Teens on Thursday afternoons.

    Teens Thu 16:00

    Book CrossFit Kids

Also on the timetable: Peach Camp, Team WOD on Saturday, Mobility on Sunday and Open Gym most afternoons.

The coaches

Owner · CrossFit Level 2

FX trained in Australia (Certificate III and IV in Sport and Fitness), the UK (Level 3 Personal Training) and France (BPJEPS Sport and Weightlifting), and holds Les Mills Grit. His favourite part is watching members push through a hard session.

CrossFit Kids · Hybrid training

  • Functional & hybrid
  • CrossFit Kids
  • Weight management

Annika coaches members of every age, from CrossFit Kids to adults working on weight management. Expect a lot of energy in her classes.

Calisthenics · Handstands

  • Calisthenics
  • Handstand
  • Muscle gain

Hakim teaches calisthenics, handstands and muscle building, starting from technique so you train without getting hurt.

Chinese weightlifting

  • Weightlifting
  • Technique

Hansen has more than 10 years in Chinese weightlifting under Coach Jianping Ma, a former Chinese national athlete, and co-founded Ma Weightlifting in 2022. He is a biomechanics engineer and teaches beginners a clean foundation. His class runs Saturday at 15:30.

Sport and Health Science · CrossFit Level 1

  • Functional training
  • CrossFit for beginners
  • Weightlifting
  • Fat loss

Palm started training to get stronger after getting sick often. He coaches gymnastics, weightlifting and weight loss, and spends most of his time on getting your technique right.

Sports Science, Chulalongkorn · Pilates

  • Functional training
  • Pilates
  • CrossFit

Sompriaw has coached functional fitness for over 6 years and CrossFit for 2, and teaches Pilates. She plans training around moving better first and getting stronger on top of it.
